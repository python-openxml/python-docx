Bookmark fixture
================

``bookmarks-word.docx`` was authored with Microsoft Word on Windows.
It contains a run-range bookmark named Intro, collapsed targets named
Details_2 and Details_3 on two identical heading paragraphs, and an internal
hyperlink to Details_3. Word navigation was verified by moving the selection
to the document end and following the hyperlink. The selection moved to the
second Details paragraph at the bookmark's start position.

Author and last-modifier properties were cleared after fixture creation.
